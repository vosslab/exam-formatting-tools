16. The pH of a sample of blood is 7.4, while gastric juice is pH 1.4. The blood sample has:

  A) 6 times higher [H+] than the gastric juice. B) 6 times lower [H+] than the gastric juice.

  C) 6,000 times lower [H+] than the gastric juice. D) 1 million times lower [H+] than the gastric juice.
