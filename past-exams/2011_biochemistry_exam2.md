```text
Presentation Multiple Choice (16 questions; 1 point each; circle the answer)

1. What cellular characteristic allowed the "pHLIP" insertion proteins to uniquely target cancer cells?

  A) Cancers cells have a unique fatty acid composition in their membrane bilayer

  B) Cancerous tumors tend to internalize more extracellular debris than healthy tissues

  C) Cancer cells have a lower extracellular pH compared to healthy tissues

  D) Tumor cancer cells contain heterogenous membrane protein binding sites

2. Which of the following is not a key feature that distinguishes cancer cells from normal tissue?

  A) increased lactate production B) increased oxygen consumption

  C) defective mitochondria D) increased glucose uptake

3. The phenomenon that distinguishes cancer cells from normal tissue (described in question #2, above) is commonly known as the:

  A) Aflatoxin effect B) Warburg effect C) Hypoxia D) Wintersteiner effect

4. Phosphorylated amino acids were incorporated into beta-hairpin structures to study the effect on its structure. Common phosphorylated amino acids include, serine, tyrosine and ______.

  A) valine B) aspartic acid C) threonine D) tryptophan

5. The proposed selective gating mechanism of the nuclear pore complex (NPC) is based on_________ and _______ interactions between the FG-domain and the transport receptors.

  A) opening and closing B) Binding and unbinding

  C) Signaling and transporting D) Stabilizing and destabilizing

6. Which of the proteins listed below, are NOT part of the RNA-induced silencing complex (RISC) involved in microRNA degradation?

  A) Argonaute (Ago2) B) Dicer endoribonuclease

  C) RNA Spliceosome D) Tar RNA binding protein (TRBP)

7. In silico using a nano-scale ____, researchers were able study the effects of both polar and non-polar (hydrophobic) confinement on protein folding.

  A) typtophan cage B) valine blob C) diamond box D) molecular tweezers

8. Circular dichroism (CD) is an effective way to measure

  A) protein hydrophobic packing B) protein ligand binding

  C) protein-RNA interactions D) protein secondary structure

9. The tyrosine kinase, Syk, involved in which of the following pathways.

  A) signal transduction B) cellular necrosis

  C) depurination of adenosine D) synthesis of tyrosine and proline

10. When blood glucose levels are high, insulin stimulates the synthesis of glycogen by _____.

  A) Activating glycogen synthase kinase B) Activating glycogen synthase

  C) Inactivating glycogen synthase kinase D) Inactivating glycogen synthase

11. Inhibition of fatty acid oxidation in leukemia cells that use an ATP uncoupling strategy should:

  A) Increase oxygen consumption and increase ATP production.

  B) Decrease oxygen consumption and decrease ATP production.

  C) Decrease oxygen consumption and leave ATP production unchanged.

  D) Increase oxygen consumption and decrease ATP production.

12. The percent functional portion of a biomolecule or substrate compared to the total amount of sample is called:

  A) Functional ratio B) Fractional velocity C) Fractional activity D) Activation coefficient

13. Ricin A-chain is a known inhibitor of:

  A) Lactase and milk production B) Ribosomes and protein synthesis

  C) Polymerase and DNA replication D) GPCR and signal transduction

14. What is the main difference between normal cells and cancerous cells in terms of ATP production?

  A) Cancerous cells depend on the citric acid cycle B) Cancerous cells produce little to no lactic acid

  C) Cancerous cells depend on enhanced glycolysis C) Cancerous cells have enhanced glycogen synthesis

15. What is Saccharopine?

  A) A metabolite found only in cancerous cells B) Dong-Jin's favorite protein

  C) A key enzyme in the production of biofuels D) An intermediate in the metabolism of lysine

16. By limiting the nitrate concentration in growth medium in green algae, what effects did it have on the total biomass and relative lipid production?

  A) Increased biomass, but decreased lipid production B) Increased biomass and increased lipid production

  C) Increased lipid production, but decreased biomass D) Decreased biomass and decreased lipid production
```

```text
Normal Multiple Choice (10 questions; 1 point each; circle the answer)

17. A strong oxidizing agent has a strong tendency to ________ electron(s).
```
