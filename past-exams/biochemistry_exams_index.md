# Biochemistry past-exam searchable text

This archive contains 29 lossy, image-free Markdown artifacts: 18 dated exam groups
with separate forms, parts and later revisions, plus three undated native sources.
The dated sources cover 2011-2013, 2017, spring/fall 2018, 2019 and 2026. An exam group
identifies an archive source; its presence does not prove which version was administered.

Exam counts follow each year's sequence. In the located sources, 2011, 2012, 2017,
2018 and 2019 have two exams; 2013 and 2026 have three. Final parts A/B and alternate
forms belong to the same exam rather than counting as additional exams. The six
Blackboard configurations described below document a three-exam sequence separately.

## Search results and gaps

The supplied `file_dump.txt` lists 379 paths. All 354 checksum rows in
`file_dump.md5sum` match their local files, representing 219 byte-unique files across
exams, grades, guides and other material. Exact copies in later annual folders do not
establish additional exams. Source mappings are retained locally in
`biochemistry_exam_sources.tsv`.

Six Blackboard Test Canvas PDFs exported October 14, 2021 are byte-identical across
2021, 2022 and both 2023 course folders. They record Exam 1A/1B, Exam 2A/2B and
Final Exam 3A/3B bank selections and points, but contain no readable prompts or choices.
They establish a three-exam configuration; underlying question-bank exports would be
needed to recover searchable exam content. These inventories are not counted above.
Their six representative PDFs are retained in `ARTIFACTS/` with `_inventory.pdf` names.
No distinct question-bearing 2020-2025 exam was established from the supplied list.

The 2011 form-B ODT was edited in 2018; its original administered state is unproven.
The 2011 exam-2 native supplies clean student text; its scanned instructor-key PDF is
retained for corroboration. The 2013 exam-2 PDF has nine pages labeled 1-9 of 10 and
an inherited 2013 title with a 2018 header date. The 2017 form-A PDF has eleven pages
labeled 1-11 of 12. Native bonus pages and changed instructions are documented in the
companions and are not silently merged into the PDFs.

The 2026 exam-1 companion follows the later May PDF. Its February original and
ODT/DOCX companions have the same questions and choices but lack one added drawing
hint in short-answer 3(d). Both PDF revisions are retained and linked in the companion.

Shorter drafts, separate exports of an already combined exam, wrapper/cheat-sheet
variants, quizzes, guides, grade records and inherited Seiser material are outside this
question-bearing exam selection. The unversioned chapters 1-5 DOCX and its duplicate
are superseded by the retained v2 draft. Original source files remain unchanged.

## Conversion and validation

PDF companions use `pdftotext -layout`, selected after bounded raw-text comparisons
to preserve choices and table rows. ODT/DOCX companions use native text extraction.
Semantic headings and fixed-width blocks make text searchable; PDF page locators are
navigation aids. Unicode scientific notation is normalized to ASCII. Figures, chemical
structures, colors, exact layout, object-only equations and graphical answer choices
remain in the original binary sources and can limit question interpretation.

The 29 companions include 244 PDF page locators and five native-only text sources.
Checks cover one H1 per file, balanced fences, ASCII text, no embedded images, source
links, extracted-text retention and byte-identical new source copies. The book delivery
validator's filename convention is superseded by the requested lower snake_case names.
Conversion, source-comparison and checksum receipts are retained in ignored
`output_biochemistry_archive_search/` and `output_biochemistry_markdown/`.
